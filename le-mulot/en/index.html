Le Mulot

Your walks, your own map of Paris.

Le Mulot remembers the Paris streets you explore on foot and colours them as you return. Revisit your days in the Journal, see your progress and leave a note on places that matter.

This version covers Paris. Background recording uses location and motion activity with your permission. No Le Mulot account is needed.
